    A proctor credit is used when a candidate takes a proctored exam. A proctored exam is when an instructor or admin is able to monitor the exam live by viewing the candidate while they’re taking the exam. If a group of 10 candidates is taking a proctored exam, then 10 proctor credits get used in addition to 10 exam credits. One exam credit and one proctor credit are required per candidate per proctored exam attempt.
    An exam credit is used when a candidate takes an exam. One exam credit is required per candidate per exam attempt. When a plan is purchased with SpeedExam, exam credits are allotted for a month or a year depending on the selected plan, and if the exam is for a group of 10 candidates, then 10 exam credits get used.
    We have three different types of proctoring available with us.
    ProctorLite - This type of proctoring is image-based. Candidates’ images are captured live during an exam depending on the frequency chosen by the admin/instructor. (If the admin chooses 5 minutes, then an image is captured once every 5 minutes.)
    Audio & Video Based Proctoring - This type of proctoring captures audio and video feeds of candidates while they’re taking the exam. The entire video is also recorded and available for viewing later if the exam cannot be monitored live. There is an option of terminating the exam in case a candidate is found indulging in unusual behaviour like copy-pasting, print-screening, opening a new tab, minimising the browser, etc.
    AI-Based Audio & Video Proctoring - This type of proctoring is highly sensitive, and it is AI-monitored. If a candidate is found indulging in unusual behavior, the AI auto-terminates the exam. The video and audio feed is recorded and available for viewing later if the exam cannot be monitored live.
